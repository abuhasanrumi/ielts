Examiners score fluency and coherence, lexical resource, grammar, pronunciation. They do not score truth. A well-told invented story scores the same as a true one.
"

Memorized answers fail because examiners randomize combinations. Pre-write "describe a helpful person" and draw "describe someone who taught you something." That script breaks. A small set of flexible ideas holds. Know the person, place, or event well and angle it toward the card in hand.

02 · Method

The reusable idea-generation framework

The instruction is simple. Fix one real idea per category. Note its strengths and one extraordinary detail. Arrive ready. The six lenses below turn those notes into who, what, when, where, why and feeling for a full two-minute answer. Step through them.

03 · Topic bank
